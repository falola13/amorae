Amorae · design direction · round 3

The shared book

Amorae is set like a book the two of you keep: one paper, one iron-gall ink, one plum rubric. The app speaks in a quiet sans; the things you wrote are set in a text serif. Sections are separated the way a book does it — rules, a change of paper, the type itself. The menu is the page’s foot, and the tab you are on is marked with a ribbon bookmark.

Menu bar

The foot of the page: same stock, one hairline, no fill, no blur, nothing floating. The current tab carries a plum ribbon bookmark hanging from the hairline, an ink label at a heavier weight, and a heavier icon stroke — three cues, only one of them colour. Every tab is a 64px-tall, ~76px-wide target above the safe-area inset.

From lg, the side nav is the book’s contents page: wordmark, the couple as a running head, five entries, and a colophon at the foot. The ribbon turns sideways and points in from the spine. Between md and lg the rail is the phone tab stood on end.

Home

One raised leaf — Ada’s request, because someone is waiting on it. The sealed question is a change of paper with an envelope flap. The countdown and the prayer list are only rules and type.

Our space (Together)

A contents page, not a launcher. The groups are italic section heads with plain ruled rows under them. No leading icons, because the words are enough and the chevron says “opens”. Our story sits in the middle as a chapter opening with the latest entry, since it is the one place the couple reads rather than does.

Our story

A tab, so there is no back button; the masthead is the title. A chapter per month: a heavy ink rule and the month in the serif. Each entry hangs off a date in the margin. A photograph gets the full column. The goals strip is set in ruled newspaper columns that run off the edge, because it scrolls.

Prayer mode

Full screen, so no menu bar: the page changes to paper and the chrome falls to three controls. The prayer is set at reading size in the serif, and the scripture reference is the rubric, in plum.
